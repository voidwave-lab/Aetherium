# Aetherium // Radical Financial Transparency & Zero-Profit Ledger (`voidwave-lab`)

**Aetherium** is developed and maintained by **[voidwave-lab](https://github.com/voidwave-lab)** as a public-benefit, open-source sanctuary for deep listening, neurodivergent sensory regulation, and receptive music therapy.

---

## 1. The Zero-Profit Pledge

1. **100% Free & Libre Forever:** Aetherium is released under the **GNU General Public License v3.0 (`GPL-3.0-or-later`)**. Neither the Desktop clinical suite nor **Aetherium Pocket** (PWA, iOS, Android) will ever contain paywalls, subscriptions, in-app purchases, advertisements, or locked acoustic seeds.
2. **Zero Telemetry & Zero Surveillance:** Aetherium operates 100% offline. We collect zero analytics, zero crash dumps, zero user identifiers, and zero behavioral data.
3. **Zero Personal Profit:** `voidwave-lab` takes **$0.00** in developer compensation or personal profit from community donations.

---

## 2. Why Community Support Exists (`patreon.com/voidwavelab`)

While hosting source code on GitHub, publishing Desktop binaries (macOS, Windows, Linux), deploying the PWA on GitHub Pages, and distributing Android `.apk` builds on GitHub Releases / F-Droid are **100% free**, distributing native iOS builds on the **Apple App Store & TestFlight** requires a mandatory **$99.00 USD / year Apple Developer Program membership fee** (plus optional Google Play $25 one-time registration or domain costs).

- **Community Support Link:** [https://patreon.com/voidwavelab](https://patreon.com/voidwavelab)
- **Strict Allocation Rule:**
  1. Community contributions are used **strictly** to pay the mandatory annual platform distribution and signing fees (such as the $99/year Apple Developer Program fee) so iOS users and clinicians can install Aetherium effortlessly without needing Xcode.
  2. **100% of Surplus Donated Forward:** At the end of each calendar year, every dollar collected above our verified infrastructure receipts is donated directly to **mental health non-profits**, **music therapy foundations**, and **open-source software/audio collectives** (such as the Electronic Frontier Foundation, Processing Foundation, or Linux Audio developers).

## 4. GPLv3 Section 7 Mobile App Store Distribution Exception

To reconcile strict copyleft freedom (`GPL-3.0-or-later`) with the technical digital-signature constraints of mobile app stores (specifically the Apple App Store and TestFlight), `voidwave-lab` grants an explicit **GPLv3 Section 7 Additional Permission** in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) permitting the distribution of unmodified and modified binaries of Aetherium through the Apple App Store, Apple TestFlight, Google Play Store, and F-Droid, provided the complete corresponding source code remains publicly accessible under `GPL-3.0-or-later`.
